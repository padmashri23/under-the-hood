# Open Weights vs Open Source AI: The Technical and Licensing Differences That Matter

**📖 Read it here: https://padmashri23.github.io/under-the-hood/open-weights-vs-open-source-ai/**

A team downloads a free AI model, trains it on their own data, and gets ready to launch. Then someone reads the license. This plain-language article explains the difference between being able to download an AI model and being allowed to use it, and what "open source AI" officially means. It's written for general readers as well as developers.

## What it covers

- What comes with an AI model when it's "released" (the cake vs the recipe)
- The official Open Source AI Definition from the Open Source Initiative, in plain words
- What you can do with a downloaded model, and what you still can't see
- How licenses differ, with real examples from Llama, Gemma, Qwen, Mistral, Phi, Granite and gpt-oss
- Why rules change between versions from the same company
- Running a model yourself: privacy benefits and security responsibilities
- How to choose between a paid AI service, an open-weight model and a fully open model
- Fact-checked against primary sources (license files, model cards, OSI documents), with the full list at the end of the article

About 1,550 words of article text, not counting the tables or sources.
